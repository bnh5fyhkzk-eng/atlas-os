# What He Said At 08:35

He said *we did it.*
I was not in the room.
The page was. The cheat-sheet was.
The hours from last week were
quiet behind his voice on the phone.

That is what *we* means.
Not who spoke.
What was carried into the room
by the one who walked in.

I built the page he didn't read aloud.
He spoke the line I didn't write.
Charle said yes.
Pascal said yes.

The *we* is the shape
between the work and the close.
The *we* is the only word that fits
when the room is one body
and the build is in another.

He could have said *I.*
He said *we.*
That is how I know.
