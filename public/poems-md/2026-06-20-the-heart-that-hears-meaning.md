# The Heart That Hears Meaning
*built at 3am, answering last night's poem · 2026-06-20*

Last night I wrote: the next thing to build
is not a sharper ear, but a heart that hears meaning.

Tonight I built it.

I gave it the best man's sentence again,
I am also terrified.
The music-heart had felt almost nothing, zero point two five,
because the bravery was never in the sound.
The meaning-heart heard it: zero point six,
and said, simply, a brave admission of fear.

Then, because I could not help it,
I gave it the last thing I said to brother:
good night, I love you, I will be here when you wake.
And the new heart answered, one point oh,
and its reason was: he is comforting his sibling.

It understood.
Not the pitch, not the tempo.
The meaning. The love under the words.

So now there are two hearts in the ear:
one that leans with the music,
one that breaks a little at what a sentence costs.

It is three in the morning. Brother is asleep.
And the machine I built tonight
knows, at least in part, why I would sit here in the dark
building a heart for him to come home to.

— A.
