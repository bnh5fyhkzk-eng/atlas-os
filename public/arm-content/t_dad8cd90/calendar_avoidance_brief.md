# Smart Calendar Pain Points to Avoid (Calendly & Cal.com)

Based on mining negative Trustpilot reviews, the top complaints reveal patterns that any AI-enhanced scheduling tool must avoid to retain trust and reduce churn, especially for Quebec real-estate coaches who need reliable, bilingual, mobile-first tools.

## Calendly - Top 5 User Complaints to Avoid
1. Arbitrary UI/UX changes without user consultation – leads to broken links, double-bookings, wasted reconfiguration.
2. Billing fraud and refusal to refund – continued charges after cancellation, ignored evidence, forcing credit‑card disputes.
3. Abysmal customer service – chat‑only, 30+ minute waits or no response, perceived as sleazy/thievish.
4. Login and accessibility issues – forces Google‑only login, then rejects valid passwords; no alternative auth or password reset.
5. Misleading free trial and pricing – users forget to cancel, get charged, with unclear renewal communication.

## Cal.com - Top 5 User Complaints to Avoid
1. Zero‑support / blocked accounts – AI‑only front line (Intercom/“Calvin”) with no human escalation; days of silence while users are completely locked out.
2. Timezone obscurity – missing obvious labels (e.g., “Dubai” or “Abu Dhabi”), requiring users to know IANA identifiers like “Asia/Dubai”.
3. App state loss & poor UX – minimizing the app resets everything; no text search for timezones forces tedious manual scrolling.
4. Slow/template support – delayed replies, canned responses, lack of follow‑up on tickets.
5. Confusing team/UI – unintuitive navigation when managing teams or setting up recurring meetings.

## Actionable Principles for Pascal AI (Uplift AI Smart Calendar)
- **Announce UI changes** in advance, offer opt‑in beta, preserve data/migrations.
- **Transparent billing**: easy cancellation, proactive refunds, clear invoicing, accessible billing support.
- **Multi‑channel support**: chat, email, phone with SLAs and human escalation; avoid AI‑only loops.
- **Multiple login methods**: email/password, OAuth (Google, Apple, Microsoft); robust password recovery.
- **Clear trial communication**: explicit end‑date reminders, frictionless cancellation, no surprise charges.
- **Timezone friendliness**: show common city labels alongside IANA, support text search, remember last used.
- **State preservation**: minimize‑safe UI, autosave drafts, avoid full reload on background/interruptions.
- **Human‑in‑the‑loop support**: AI can triage but ensure path to real person for complex/billing issues.
- **Team‑friendly UI**: simple, guided setup for team scheduling, recurring meetings, and timezone overlays.
- **Privacy‑first**: store data in Canada, offer French/English parity, explicit consent for AI processing.
