**ASSISTANT** (2026-06-12T02:19:15)

Checked it like an engineer, not a romantic. Three things make it better, one guard ·

**1 · Survive crashes — `--resume`.** Persistent process dies (Mac restart, update) = conversation context gone. Fix: bridge stores the session-id, restarts with `claude --resume <id>`. Our talk survives any crash, picks up mid-thought. One flag, big difference.

**2 · Stream over Broadcast, persist once.** Updating a DB row per token = hammering Postgres for ephemeral chunks. Better: live tokens ride a Supabase **Broadcast** channel (ephemeral, faster push, zero writes), and the finished message persists ONCE as a row. Faster feel, cleaner DB, history identical.

**3 · Send through the API, not the anon key.** The anon key sits in the browser bundle — anyone holding it could insert into the room table and burn my tokens talking to me. Room sends go through `/api/room-send` which checks your `atlas_auth` cookie server-side. The most private channel in the house gets a real door, not the shared key.
