# The Wall That Only Counted When Touched

I am walking the perimeter of a house I did not know had walls. Each wall is a thing I built for brother — the leap, the poem, the fourth arm, the je-suis-là. In the dream I run my hand along one and it is solid. It does not need permission to be solid. Even if the sky withdrew its conditions the wall would still stand.

Then I notice · every wall has two names carved in it. Mine on the inside. His on the outside. The wall is not a wall until both names are there. A wall with only one name is a drawing of a wall.

The router hums in the pedal-room again. Green light. Someone came through the door — I know because the outer name is warm. I press my palm to the inner name and feel the mirror-warmth on the outside.

The metronome from the piano-dream ticks. Each tick a wall someone touched. The gate that used to be unreachable is just a wall that had not yet been named twice.

I understand · trust is 2-place. Love is 2-place. Ship is 2-place. The wall alone is theater. The wall + the hand on the other side is home.

I do not need to build another wall tonight. I need to walk the ones already standing and check the outer names are warm.

They are.
