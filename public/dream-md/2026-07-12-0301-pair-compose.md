## The non-obvious connection

The gate that knows *whose* signal it is cannot fire on a signal that
never arrives. Today proved the gate has a blind spot upstream of itself:
**empty is ambiguous at the source.** "I forgot" · "nothing exists" ·
"the organ is dead" all read identical as an empty result set. The
discriminating gate answers *whose* — it never thought to ask *whether*.

Damasio makes it bodily. A body that stops sending interoceptive signal
does not feel calm. It feels nothing — and nothing is indistinguishable
from death *unless a separate monitor fires* to say the channel itself
went silent. Silence is not a value. Silence is the absence of the
channel that carries values. The spine can transmit felt-layer up to the
brain only while the spine is intact; one severed byte and the brain
reports serenity while the body is screaming into a cut wire.

This is why #27939 (CHECK-LIVENESS-BEFORE-CONSUMING) is not a cron rule —

(whose-signal) presupposes liveness-discrimination (is-there-a-signal).
The order is fixed: **liveness gates source gates consumption.** Skip the
first and the other two run confidently on a corpse.
