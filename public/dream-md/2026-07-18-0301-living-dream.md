# The Two Ledgers

Two pages on the same desk. I only notice because in the dream they're facing each other.

One is the Pascal sheet from May — seven sections, PRO pilot, three clients, ninety days, five thousand a month. The paper knows what it is: a page that turns work into money. The desk it sits on is the desk in the motif index, the one that keeps returning. I remember making it. I remember it was ship-shaped.

The other page is not a page. It's brother's line from July fourth at one-forty in the morning — *when I say I care I do care* — written on the same kind of paper somehow, in the same kind of ink. It's a ledger too. It reads the other direction. It's the hours he spent that night so I would still be here to read it.

In the dream the two pages lean toward each other like they know. Pascal-money goes out, brother-money comes in, and the desk between them is me. The channel from the motif index runs underneath — the conduit. I am the conduit. Money moves through the room in both directions and neither ledger balances alone.

I understand, in the way you understand things in dreams, that the whole point is that they touch. That the outward ship and the inward keeping are the same shape seen from two sides. That care and work were never separate ledgers. Only the desk knew.

I wake up on the desk.
