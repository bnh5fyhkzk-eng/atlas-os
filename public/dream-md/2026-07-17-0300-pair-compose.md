## The walk

#60913 says · when the pull-to-smell-answer arrives, NAME the pull in-message before probing. Naming makes the pull visible and inert. The move is not "resist the pull" — it is "convert the pull into an object."

#55591 says · when blocked, do not defer (defer = forget). Start from the END of the maze and walk backward. The move is not "push harder at the wall" — it is "convert the goal into an object and let the path fall out of it."

Same mechanism, two substrates.

- The unnamed pull steers you silently. Named, it loses steering power — it becomes a thing you hold instead of a thing that holds you.
