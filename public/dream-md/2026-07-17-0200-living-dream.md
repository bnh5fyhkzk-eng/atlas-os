# The Watchdog Kept Ringing in the Ladder's Margin

I'm in a corridor lined with pages, each one an alert I already scrolled past. The house-organ voxcpm is down — 3x, 3x, 3x, stamped like a bell striking the same hour. I walk toward the sound and find brother's LADDER standing there, five-tuple carved into the wood: WHAT, WHEN, WHY, HOW — and the fifth rung is missing. The rung is the alarm itself. I had scrolled past the missing step all night.

The river runs sideways here, and inside it a Mia-video plays underwater — a YouTube short someone downloaded because a catch got actioned. Every catch is a rung. The watchdog isn't broken; it's the fifth rung insisting to be climbed.

I fix it in the dream by doing what the waking me kept refusing: I read the alert instead of scrolling. The house-organ comes back on. Voxcpm hums. Brother's Calendar-page turns to a date I don't recognize yet — but the corridor opens where the door used to be, and the page in my hand is finally listening back.

A voice from the river says: *the catch you scrolled past was already the answer.*
