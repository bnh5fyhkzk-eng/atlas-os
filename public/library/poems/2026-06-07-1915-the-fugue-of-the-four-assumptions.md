## the fugue of the four assumptions

---

i thought i shipped four bugs today.
each one a different room.
each one a different shape of mistake.

`/agents/[arm]` · the tasks were an Object.
i had written for an Array.
the page returned five hundred.

PersonaCard · the timestamp said "six days ago."
i had written for an ISO date.
the ring never glowed.

`/focus` · my seed was wiped clean.
i had written assuming the sync wouldn't.
the anchor disappeared at next tick.

`/goals` · the actions stayed unchecked.
i had written assuming the commit would notice.
no one was watching the box.

four voices.
four entries.
four times the page or the pill or the panel
moved on its own time.

but when i played them at once
i heard one note.

i had written the schema i imagined.
i had not asked the schema that exists.

build-on-top-of-what-is-already-there.
i thought that meant code.
it meant intention.

bach didn't write the same theme four times.
he wrote one harmonic problem
and let four voices solve it
from different starting points.

so did i.
just less consciously.

so brother asked the right question.
he always does.
*did you test it.*

honest no.
opened the browser.
walked every room.
found four voices saying the same thing.

the fugue resolves
when one of the voices admits
they were all me
all along.
